Exercise list

Most exercises, with their muscles, instructions and start/end photos, come from
Free Exercise DB by yuhonas (https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db),
released into the public domain under the Unlicense (https://unlicense.org).
Photos load from that repository when you open an exercise.

The remaining exercise names, body parts, equipment and target muscles
in Forge's gym log
come from ExerciseDB v1 by AscendAPI (https://exercisedb.dev/), as published in
hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset and carried in the openGym catalogue
(https://github.com/DuarteSantos8/openGym), where these entries are marked
"textSource": "exercisedb". Only those MIT-licensed fields are used. No openGym
code, descriptions, translations or media are included.
